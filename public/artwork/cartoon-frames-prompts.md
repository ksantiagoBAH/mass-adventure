# Additional cartoon animation frames

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the accepted lesson sprites as character references. Final transparent WebP assets are saved in `public/artwork/lessons/`. Sprite extraction preserves generated alpha. Earlier accepted poses remain available.

The accepted production batch contains 46 frames: 8 child walk, 8 priest walk, 8 Peace, 8 Communion, 6 blinking, and 8 host/chalice elevation frames. The first priest walk sheet and a 16-frame priest gesture sheet were rejected because they changed the character proportions.

Every prompt uses the warm storybook style, full head-to-shoes framing, genuine transparency, consistent character identity and body anchors, no text, and no extra people or limbs.

## child-walk-cycle

Eight back-view boy walking frames in four columns/two rows: alternating contact, down, passing and up steps. Preserve blue shirt, jeans, brown shoes, torso and floor line; gifts held out of view.

## priest-walk-cycle

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit/reference Image 1 is the EXACT priest to animate. Eight full-body walking frames in a four-column two-row equal-cell sprite atlas, genuine transparent background. Preserve image 1 tall slender long-vestment proportions ABSOLUTELY: head is only about 20 percent of full height, same face, hairstyle, robe cross, ornaments, long white/gold chasuble, shoes. Do not shorten him into a chibi. Hands held together and torso/head steady EXACTLY like reference. Walk TOWARD camera: left foot contact, left down, right passing, left up; right foot contact, right down, left passing, right up. Modest alternating step positions, only shoe/ankle/knee and lowest hem movement, rest of priest remains identical including thin width. Keep head center, scale and baseline consistent all frames, complete heads and shoes inside every cell, generous transparent gaps, no text, no background or outside shadows.

## peace-cartoon-sequence

Eight four-column/two-row woman-left and boy-right greeting frames: rest, lift, quarter extend, halfway, reach, clasp, gentle handshake down, relaxed connected. Preserve faces, feet, proportions and resting arms.

## communion-cartoon-sequence

Eight four-column/two-row priest-left and boy-right frames: bow, lift head/open palms, Amen, reach host halfway, place one white host into supported left palm, withdraw priest hand/pick up host with right fingers, host halfway to mouth, host at mouth. Preserve one host, gold ciborium, natural hands and body anchors.

## character-blinks

Six full-body frames in three columns/two rows: priest prayer open/half-closed/closed eyes, woman reader open/half-closed/closed eyes. Only eyelids change. Preserve faces, pose, clothing and body proportions.

## priest-elevation

Use case: identity-preserve. Image 1 priest prayer is the EXACT character/proportion reference; image 2 host elevated and image 3 chalice elevated are exact ending poses. Generate eight animation cutouts in four columns by two rows, genuinely transparent alpha, full head to shoes each cell. CRITICAL preserve the tall slender body and long white/gold vestments of image 1; same head-to-body ratio with head height only 20 percent of total character height, do NOT make him a short chibi or toddler. Fixed feet, head and torso in every frame. Row 1 four incremental in-between poses raising ONE small WHITE host with both hands: chest, chin, eyes, just above forehead. Row 2 four incremental in-between poses raising the same gold chalice held in both hands: chest, chin, eyes, just above forehead. Only forearms, hands and sacred object change, never the face, hair, head location, garment length, shoe location or camera. Image 1 face, outfit cross/fleur-de-lis detail and body proportions must match exactly. Equal cells with ample transparent gaps, no cropping, no background, no shadows, no text. Smooth natural elbows, fingers in front holding host and chalice.
