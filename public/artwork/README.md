## Animated lesson priest

The five `lessons/priest-*.webp` cutouts were generated with the built-in image tool using the existing procession priest as the character and style reference. They depict prayer, elevation of the host, elevation of the chalice, the doxology, and blessing. Unused seated and offertory character variants are archived locally in `output/lesson-animations/assets-archive` rather than included in the site.

The lesson scenes reuse `parish-sanctuary-paten.webp` and the procession church background. Pages are drawn as SVG leaves turning around a fixed spine; the covers stay on the ambo. Each scene ends in a still pose, supports replay, and uses a still ending when reduced motion is requested. Foreground children are omitted from Mass scenes at the user's request; the separate Sign of the Cross practice retains its character.

Listening lessons 1–3 use the matching adult woman reader cutout (`lessons/reader.webp`). Reading scenes use `lessons/sanctuary-no-book.webp`, edited with the built-in image tool to remove the baked-in Bible from the ambo. The animated thin book shows its exterior covers to the congregation; the leaf turns around the fixed spine. Gospel and homily retain the priest.

Presentation of the Gifts restores the matching front/back child sprites for the approach and turn. `lessons/child-gifts-wine.webp` was generated from the bread bearer as a character reference, replacing the plate and bread with a wine cruet. Both bearers keep their gifts in the final congregation-facing pose.

Presentation of the Gifts uses the wide procession church background, with the priest fully visible in front of the altar and the gift bearers approaching through the aisle before turning.

Sign of Peace uses a generated two-pose woman/child greeting atlas based on the existing reader and gift bearer. Holy Communion uses a generated three-pose priest/recipient atlas: bow, Amen with open hands, and host received. Both reuse the procession church, support replay and reduced motion, and hold the ending. Gospel lesson 5 restores the matching thumb cutout tracing small crosses on forehead, lips and heart with the existing articulated practice character.

The Communion ending adds `communion-mouth.webp`, edited from the received-host pose to show the boy bringing the Eucharist to his mouth with his right hand. Gospel small crosses draw their vertical and horizontal strokes in sync with the thumb at each location.

Thirteen additional intermediate poses were generated from the accepted character cutouts: two peace-greeting poses, three Communion transition poses, four gift-bearer quarter turns, and four priest arm/vessel poses. Pose sequences retain fixed floor anchors, preload their SVG image frames, interpolate adjacent poses, and use isolated additive blending to keep overlapping bodies opaque. The gift bearers turn through drawn side views instead of horizontal flattening.

The offertory paten now carries one thin white host in the front-facing and both quarter-turn poses, replacing the earlier loaf. All three were edited together from their existing sprites; previous loaf assets are archived locally. Lamb of God adds matching SVG fingers in front of each host half, moving with the halves as they separate.
